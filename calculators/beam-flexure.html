Method note. This tool applies the ACI 440.2R strain-compatibility procedure for flexural strengthening with externally bonded FRP: internal compressive and tensile forces are iterated to equilibrium at a converged neutral-axis depth c, from which εfe, εc, εs, and the design flexural strength ϕMn are derived. Effective depth accounts for the stirrup (d = h − cc − Østirrup − Ømain/2), and reinforcement is entered as a discrete bar count rather than a spacing-based strip, consistent with a beam section.

The eligibility check (ACI 440.2R §9.2) confirms the beam can carry its anticipated service loads even with a complete, unanticipated loss of the FRP system: the existing (unstrengthened) capacity ϕMn0 is computed with the standard ACI 318 Whitney stress block, and compared against 1.1MDL + 0.75MLL (or +1.0MLL when the live load is sustained long-term). The same strain-based ϕ transition (0.65–0.90, between εt = fy/Es and 0.005) is applied to both the existing and CFRP-strengthened sections. Existing soffit strain εbi and the service-level stress/creep-rupture checks use standard elastic cracked-section (transformed, steel-only) properties.

Entering Mu as a negative value treats it as hogging: the tension reinforcement switches to the top bars for the CFRP-strengthened check, the existing-capacity/eligibility check, and the effective depth, which is then measured from the soffit up to the top-bar centroid.

This tool is intended for preliminary design and verification. Confirm results against ACI 440.2R and a licensed engineer's independent review before construction.
