Method note. This mirrors the workbook's Axial&Moment tab exactly: Points A, B and C are computed in closed form per ACI 440.2R Appendix D (Eq. D-1 through D-3i), not by a numerical strain sweep. Per that appendix's own title, this is a simplified, partial interaction diagram — only three points (A, B, and the balanced-failure point C) are computed and connected with straight lines; it is not the full, continuously curved P-M envelope, and demand points that fall outside the A-B-C range aren't covered by this method. The steel contribution at each point sums every physical bar layer — 2 corner bars + intermediate-x bars on whichever pair of faces is perpendicular to bending, plus the intermediate-y bars on the other two faces, evenly spaced between them — rather than a single representative layer, so it stays exact for any bar count and either bending axis. Step 7's plies estimate is an axial-only design check (it ignores Mu entirely) carried over from the spreadsheet's own parallel calculation; it is not what sizes the strengthened curve above, which always uses the "Number of plies installed" you specify.
