Method note. Vc and Vs are computed using the full flexural effective depth, d = h − cover, per standard ACI 318 practice. Only the CFRP contribution, Vf, uses the reduced depth dfv based on the height actually accessible to the wrap — this keeps the existing concrete and steel shear capacity from being understated when the FRP can't reach the full beam depth (e.g. a slab bearing on top). For a Full Wrap, εfe is taken directly as min(0.75εfu, 0.004) since fully anchored wraps aren't bond-critical; U-Wrap and two-side schemes use the bond-dependent coefficient κv (capped at 0.75) with k2 based on one or two active bond lengths respectively.
