Method note. This tool works the ACI 440.2R strain-compatibility procedure directly: it iterates the depth to the neutral axis, c, until internal compressive and tensile forces balance, using a Todeschini-type concrete stress block (β1, α1 derived from ε'c and εc). That live iteration replaces the single goal-seek value frozen in the original spreadsheet cell, so results stay consistent whenever the inputs above change. CE, creep-rupture, and Es values follow the properties entered in your workbook's Flexure-Slab tab. When Mu is entered negative, the tool automatically switches the tension reinforcement to the top bars and re-derives d as the distance from the soffit up to the top-bar centroid — the same procedure above, just fed from the top-bar geometry instead of the bottom bars.
